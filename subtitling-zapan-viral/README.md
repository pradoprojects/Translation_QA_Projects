# 🎬 Case Study: Multimedia Localization & Subtitling (Viral Content)

## Project Overview
This project features the English localization and subtitling of a viral Brazilian video ("Zapan"). The goal was to adapt the spontaneous, comedic dialogue between a toddler and his mother while preserving the "Character Voice" and the specific cultural nuances of the original humor.

### 🛠 Technical Specs
* **Source Language:** Portuguese (BR)
* **Target Language:** English (US)
* **Tools Used:** Canva (Video Editing), Manual Time-syncing.
* **Format:** 1920x1080 (Landscape) with vertical video overlay to ensure readability.

---

## 🧠 Localization Strategy (The "Why")

### 1. Character Voice: "Zapan"
* **Challenge:** The child mispronounces "Japão" (Japan) as "Zapan". 
* **Solution:** I chose to maintain the phonetic misspelling as **"Zapan"** in English. This preserves the "cuteness factor" and the specific speech patterns common in early childhood, which are central to the video's appeal.

### 2. Pragmatic Adaptation: The "Mommy" Dilemma
* **Challenge:** The mother asks: *"Você prefere [...] ir pro Japão... ou a mamãe?"*. A literal translation ("Do you prefer Japan or mommy?") would work, but it loses the rhythmic punch.
* **Solution:** Translated as **"Would you rather [...] go to Japan... or a mommy?"**. The addition of the indefinite article ("a mommy") emphasizes the absurd "choice" being offered, heightening the comedic timing of the child's blunt refusal.

### 3. Visual & UX Decisions
* **Top-Aligned Subtitles:** Since the source video had hardcoded Portuguese subtitles at the bottom, I positioned the localized English text at the top. This prevents visual clutter and ensures a smooth reading experience (UX).
* **Cultural Context:** Preserved the "Directed by Robert B. Weide" meme at the end to maintain the original viral "vibe," followed by professional localization credits.
* **Emphasis & Inflection:** I used full capitalization for key terms -- e.g., "WITHOUT" -- to reflect the child's emphatic tone and the comedic gravity he places on the "consequences" of his mother choosing Japan.
